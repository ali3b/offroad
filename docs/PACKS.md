# Off-road stadium racer: Synty buy list

Research date 2026-10-21. Prices are USD from syntystore.com product pages fetched that day. Every page showed a "Sold out" badge. That looks like a Shopify display quirk on digital goods, but check checkout before relying on it.
Owned packs are excluded. Asset names are quoted from each page's "Pack Contents" section.

## Top 3 (recommended first)

### 1. POLYGON - Pro Racer: MUST
- URL: https://syntystore.com/products/polygon-pro-racer
- Price: $199.99 (one-time licence)
- Size: 9 vehicles, 12 rigged characters, 800+ prefabs, plus 350+ generic prefabs. Mecanim rig, **no animations included**.
- Gaps it fills (one pack covers most of the list):
  - Grandstands / seating: "modular grandstand pieces including a VIP box", "bleachers", "railing", "tower" (timing tower stand-in)
  - Start/finish: "gantries", "traffic lights" (start lights), "flags", "bunting", "billboards", "signs", "letters, numbers, symbols", "screens"
  - Pit / furniture: "pit arms", "pit boxes", "pit wall", "raceboxes", "fuel rig", "fuel cans", "front jack", "jack stand", "air gun", "tents", "umbrellas", "barriers", "cones", "tyres" (to stack into tyre walls), "turnstiles", "ticket booth", "food cart", "foodtrucks", "portaloo", "windsock", "cameras", "speakers", "blimp", "hot air balloon"
  - Trophy / podium: "trophies", "podium", "car podiums", "medals" (attachment), FX "Bottle spray" and "confetti"
  - Crowd: characters "Crowd", "Announcers", "Drivers", "Pit Crew"; attachments "foam fingers", "snacks", "headsets", "helmets"; FX "crowd groups" and "crowd singles" (cheap crowd fill for the stands)
  - Vehicles: "truck", "rally", "dirtbike", "pace car", "trailer", with "various customizable pieces" (liveries, modular parts)
  - FX for the game feel: "wheel dust", "skid smoke", "flame booster" (nitro visual), "exhaust backfire", "impact sparks", "checkpoint", "direction arrows"
  - Environment: "ramps" (jumps), "sandpits", "tyre marks", "hills", "cliffs", "road markings"
- Not on the page: mud, a checkered start line (build it from "road markings" or a decal), "flag man" (pose a Crowd or Announcer character holding "flags").

### 2. POLYGON - Arid Desert - Nature Biome: MUST
- URL: https://syntystore.com/products/polygon-arid-desert-nature-biomes
- Price: $27.50 (50% off $54.99). It is also part of the Nature Biomes Season Two bundle ($164.97, https://syntystore.com/products/polygon-nature-biomes-season-two, with Alpine Mountain and Enchanted Forest), but buy it on its own.
- Size: 113 environment assets, 23 props, 16 FX
- Gaps it fills (desert dressing):
  - "cactus", "succulents", "tumble weed" (as a model and as an FX), "rocks", "cliffs" (mesa stand-ins), "background hills", "pebbles", "dead trees", "ground cover", "dirt roads", "bushes", "craters", "pool water and plane" (water splashes)
  - FX: "dust blowing", "dust spots", "wind streaks", "vultures", "sunray", "smoke cloud"
- Not on the page: sand dune meshes as named assets (the marketing copy mentions "rolling dunes"), mesas as such, mud.

### 3. ANIMATION - Emotes and Taunts: MUST for crowd and podium (or free Mixamo instead, see below)
- URL: https://syntystore.com/products/animation-emotes-and-taunts
- Price: $59.99
- Clips: 70 POLYGON clips per gender (Masc/Femn), plus Sidekick versions. These are Synty-rig native, so no retargeting.
- Gaps it fills (standing crowd and winner celebrations):
  - Clapping: `A_POLY_EMOT_Celebrate_Clapping_Polite_Masc`, `A_POLY_EMOT_Taunt_SlowClap_Masc`
  - Cheering: `A_POLY_EMOT_Happy_ArmsRaised_Masc`, `A_POLY_EMOT_Happy_FistPump_Masc`, `A_POLY_EMOT_Celebrate_BeatChest`, `_AmpedSkitter`, `_AirGuitar`, `_FingerGuns_Double`
  - Waving: `A_POLY_EMOT_Greet_Wave_Masc`, `A_POLY_EMOT_Greet_WaveOver_Masc`
  - Dances (podium or flag man filler): `Dance_ChestPump`, `Dance_Twist`, `Dance_Spin_Slick`
- **Not included:** seated, driving or trophy-lift clips. **No Synty ANIMATION pack has seated, driving or vehicle clips.** The full ANIMATION collection is Base Locomotion, Idles (owned), Sword Combat, Bow Combat, Emotes and Taunts, and Goblin Locomotion (https://syntystore.com/collections/animation). Idles (owned, 330 clips) also has no sit or cheer clips; its closest is "Lean". Use Mixamo for seated crowds and drivers.

## Nice-to-have

### 4. POLYGON - Apocalypse Wasteland: NICE (monster trucks / buggies)
- URL: https://syntystore.com/products/polygon-apocalypse-wasteland
- Price: $379.99 (most expensive). You own the older POLYGON Apocalypse, not this one.
- Fills: more off-road and monster trucks. Vehicles: "Hammerhead", "Ripper", "Reaper", "Roadkill", "Greaser", "Diesel", "The_Count", "Tumbler", "Scream_Roller", "Chariot", "Firefly", "Mayfly", "Whirligig", "Chopper". Page imagery calls them "apocalyptic monster trucks", "beach buggies" and "wind buggies", with a pictured "desert racetrack", "fight pit" and swamp/goop areas (a possible mud substitute).
- Caveat: it has a road-warrior look (spikes, scrap), not clean stadium pickups. Buy it only if Pro Racer's "truck" plus our City/Apocalypse vehicles don't give enough variety.

### 5. POLYGON - Farm Pack: NICE (cheap pickups and rural filler)
- URL: https://syntystore.com/products/polygon-farm-pack
- Price: $49.99
- Fills: "Pickup Truck" (one of 11 vehicles), "Hay Bales" (classic stadium track borders), "Fence sets", "Dirt Road", "Gravel Road", "Dirt Rows", "Pond". No mud named.

### 6. POLYGON - Construction Pack: NICE (lifted-pickup kit and dirt)
- URL: https://syntystore.com/products/polygon-construction-pack
- Price: $49.99
- Fills: "Pickup (plus Toolbox, Canopy, and Lightbar)", "Dirt Piles" (jump mounds), "Ground Pieces", "Dirt Roads", "Barriers", "Rocks", plus Bulldozer/Loaders as track-crew set dressing.

### 7. POLYGON - Street Racer: NICE (only if Pro Racer falls short)
- URL: https://syntystore.com/products/polygon-street-racer
- Price: $199.99
- Fills: nitro, via "Nitrous Bottles" (vehicle attachment) and FX "Exhaust Nos"; vehicles "Truck" and "Ute" with a "racing trucks body kit"; "Road Barriers/Railings (Plastic, Concrete, Steel, Tyres, etc)" (ready-made tyre barriers); "Gantries"; "Confetti Shot"; characters "Racer Female", "Racer Fan Female", "Mechanic Male/Female". No grandstands, trophy or podium. It overlaps heavily with Pro Racer, so it is low priority. Its "Nitrous Bottles" is the only named nitro canister found in any Synty pack.

### Skip / considered
- POLYGON - Western Frontier ($49.99, https://syntystore.com/products/polygon-western-frontier-pack): 147 environment and 87 prop assets, but the page names no cactus, mesa or tumbleweed. Arid Desert is the better desert buy.
- SIMPLE Racer ($29.99): a different art style (SIMPLE, not POLYGON). Don't mix it in.
- No Synty pack names **mud**. Make it ourselves: a brown wet material on the terrain plus Particle_FX (owned) splash/dust, Pro Racer "wheel dust", and Arid Desert "pool water and plane" tinted brown.

### Bundle alternative
Every page offers the **All Access Pass "starting at $30 USD/mo"**, which covers the 150+ pack library. One or two months would get Pro Racer, Arid Desert, Emotes and Taunts, Wasteland, Farm, Construction and Street Racer (about $870 bought one by one). Check the licence: keeping assets after the pass ends may require an active subscription.
